Common questions

Is mwezi free?

Opening a wallet, receiving money and sending to other mwezi users is free. Some services, like cash-out and bill-pay on Plus, have small fees. See the pricing page.

How do Pots work?

A pot is a savings goal with a name, a target and a date. Turn on auto-save and mwezi moves your chosen amount on payday, so you save before you spend.

What is a Circle?

A digital chilimba. Invite friends, family or colleagues, set the contribution and the order of payouts. mwezi collects, reminds people gently and pays out on the date.

Is my money safe?

Your wallet is protected by a PIN and face unlock, and you can freeze your card in one tap. (In a real product, funds would be held with a licensed bank and regulated by the Bank of Zambia.)

Is this a real company?

No. mwezi is a concept brand made by Mulenga Nkondwa to demonstrate brand identity, UI/UX and web design. It does not offer financial services.
