Everything in one app

Built around how money moves in a Zambian month.

Payday, rent, ZESCO, school fees, chilimba. mwezi lines them up so nothing surprises you.

Send & receive

Move kwacha to any phone number in seconds. Free between mwezi wallets, with clear fees for everything else, shown before you confirm.

Pots

Name a goal, set a date, auto-save on payday. See a calm forecast: “At K 300 a month you’ll reach it by April.”

Circles

A digital chilimba. Collects contributions, nudges gently, and pays out on time, with a record everyone can trust.

Pay bills

Electricity, water, TV, airtime and school fees in a few taps. Save your meter and account numbers once.

Virtual card

Pay online in kwacha. Freeze it, set limits and switch features on or off with one tap.

Monthly view

See where your money went by category, compared to last month, with no spreadsheets and no jargon.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is mwezi free?

Opening a wallet, receiving money and sending to other mwezi users is free. Some services, like cash-out and bill-pay on Plus, have small fees. See the pricing page.

How do Pots work?

A pot is a savings goal with a name, a target and a date. Turn on auto-save and mwezi moves your chosen amount on payday, so you save before you spend.

What is a Circle?

A digital chilimba. Invite friends, family or colleagues, set the contribution and the order of payouts. mwezi collects, reminds people gently and pays out on the date.

Is my money safe?

Your wallet is protected by a PIN and face unlock, and you can freeze your card in one tap. (In a real product, funds would be held with a licensed bank and regulated by the Bank of Zambia.)

Is this a real company?

No. mwezi is a concept brand made by Mulenga Nkondwa to demonstrate brand identity, UI/UX and web design. It does not offer financial services.
