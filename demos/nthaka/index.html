Questions, answered plainly.

Is nthaka a real company?

No. It is a concept brand created for a design portfolio. Products, prices and depots are fictional.

How do I pay?

With mobile money. You approve a prompt on your phone, then receive an SMS receipt.

Can I collect from a depot?

Yes. Depot pickup is free. Farm delivery is a flat K 60 within 40 km of a depot.

What is input credit?

A concept feature: buy inputs now and repay after harvest, with a limit based on your farm history.

nthaka is a fictional concept brand created by Mulenga Nkondwa for portfolio purposes. Not a real company; products, prices and depots are illustrative.
