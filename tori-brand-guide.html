Brand Guide

Black, gold and white on a soft grey ground. Built from the four Clearance Sale posts for iPhone 11, 13, 14 and 15, since no brand file was supplied. Hex values were sampled from the post artwork, and every rule below states what it was drawn from.

Prepared for Tori Stores · Version 1.0 · October 2026
Brand essence

Premium iPhones, plainly priced

Position

A specialist store for new iPhones in Zambia. It signals trust with receipts, warranty and swaps, and signals premium with a black and gold finish.

Personality

Confident, clean, direct. The product and the price do the talking. Nothing decorative competes with the phone.

Promise

Brand new phones, a receipt, a warranty, and the option to swap and top up. All three appear on every post.

Logo

The Tori wordmark

A heavy, wide, uppercase TORI with a phone glyph inside the O, and a spaced STORES line beneath. Two curved strokes wrap the left side like a lens arc. It is always black on a light ground.

Placement

  • Top left on every post, about 20% of the post width.
  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the O on every side.
  • Never place it on the gold gradient or over the phone image.

Handle

  • Instagram and Facebook icons followed by TORI STORES, bottom right under the phone number.
  • Always uppercase, matching the wordmark.
Colour

Four colours, one gradient

The gold only ever appears inside a black to gold gradient on headline words. It never fills a button, a box or a background. That restraint is what keeps the posts looking expensive.

About 78% white, 12% mist, 8% black type and outlines, 2% gold. The phone photograph is the only other colour on the page.

Price box rule

Boxes are white, with a thick black outline and heavy rounded corners. The Pro and Pro Max post uses a solid outline. The other three use a dashed outline. Pick one and use it everywhere. The dashed outline reads as a coupon and suits a clearance sale, so it is the recommended standard.

Voice

Short, factual, confident

Always say

BRAND NEW
Receipt & Warranty offered
Swap & Top up Allowed
CLEARANCE SALE!!

Price format

K12,499 · capital K, no space, comma at thousands, no decimals. Storage as 128gb in lowercase.

Calls to action

The phone number is the only call to action. No tag lines, no emoji, and one exclamation pair on SALE!! only.

Do

  • Lead every post with the condition: BRAND NEW.
  • Show the phone in its true colour on a clean ground.
  • Keep the footer strip identical across all posts.
  • Show a price for each storage size, smallest first.

Don't

  • Fill shapes or buttons with gold.
  • Add more than one script word to a post.
  • Put prices on a coloured or photographic ground.
  • Stretch, outline or recolour the logo.
